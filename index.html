CV – Research – Projects – Create

I'm a Predoctoral Research Fellow at Northwestern’s Kellogg School of Management, where I work primarily with Dr. Jacob Teeny and Dr. Chethana Achar. I support behavioral studies on (1) the effects of gen-AI exposure on creative self-evaluation, (2) the opposer’s loss effect in political framing, (3) identity signaling through branding, and (4) how to make political disagreement more productive. My work combines survey programming, stimulus development (narratives, graphics, mock ads), and data processing in R and SPSS.

Before Kellogg, I worked in the Social Interdependence Lab at Gonzaga University with Dr. Adam Stivers and completed my thesis with the help of Dr. Rebecca Bull Schaefer.
