CV – Research – Contact – Projects

I'm a Predoctoral Research Fellow at Northwestern’s Kellogg School of Management, where I work primarily with Jacob Teeny and Chethana Achar. My research focuses on how interpersonal goals shape emotional experiences in workplace conversations, and I’m also interested in persuasion, emotion regulation, and group dynamics.

Before Kellogg, I worked in the Social Interdependence Lab at Gonzaga University.
