My current research focuses on how interpersonal goals shape emotional experiences in workplace conversations.

Broadly, I want to study (1) how persuasive messages and social cues—like confidence, enjoyment, or identity labels—influence self-perception and decision-making, (2) how emotions are shared, regulated, and strategically expressed within groups, and (3) how everyday conversations shape interpersonal connection, motivation, and collaboration in organizations.
