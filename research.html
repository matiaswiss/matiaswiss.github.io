My current research focuses on how interpersonal goals shape emotional experiences in workplace conversations.

Broadly, I want to study how conversation shapes connection, identity, and influence. (1) How framing, disagreement, and power dynamics affect collaboration and mutual understanding (2) How people regulate emotion in group settings, and when that regulation becomes a personal cost (3) How expressions of confidence, identity, and emotion influence authenticity and social perception
