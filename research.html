Research

My current research focuses on how interpersonal goals shape emotional experiences in workplace conversations.

Research Themes

  • How persuasive messages and social cues—like confidence, enjoyment, or identity labels—influence self-perception and decision-making
  • How emotions are shared, regulated, and strategically expressed within groups
  • How everyday conversations shape interpersonal connection, motivation, and collaboration in organizations
